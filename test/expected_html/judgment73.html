By this application, a prayer has been made to stay the impugned order dated 18.06.2026 passed by the learned Civil Judge (Junior Division), East Jaintia Hills District, Khliehriat in Civil Misc.Application No.1 of 2025 arising out of Title Suit No.3 of 2025.

It is submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioner that the impugned order is contrary to the provisions of Order 1 Rule 10(2) CPC as the petitioner has been compelled to prosecute the suit filed by him jointly with a party having an adverse and rival claim in the subject matter in dispute resulting in serious prejudice to the petitioner. He submits that the learned Trial Court, by the impugned order, has placed the respondent No. 1 in the position of a co-plaintiff without appreciating the fact that the petitioner herein and respondent No. 1 have conflicting interests in the matter.
