                      Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.

Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, learned counsel for the applicant in both the applications.

Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned A.G.A. for the State of Uttarakhand.

2. The applicants, namely, Piyush Kumar in Anticipatory Bail Application No. 287 of 2026 and Amit Singh Chauhan in Anticipatory Bail Application No. 288 of 2026, seek anticipatory bail in connection with the First Information Report dated 04.06.2026, registered as FIR/Case Crime No. 0213 of 2026 at Police Station Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, for the offences punishable under Sections 111(2)(ii), 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340 and 61(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 read with Section 3/25(1B)(a) of the Arms Act, 1959.

3. Learned counsel for the applicants submitted that the only allegation attributed to the applicants in the FIR as well as during the course of investigation is that they had procured arms licences through one Saurabh Agarwal. It is further submitted that during investigation it has surfaced that Saurabh Agarwal and Satanand Sharma were the main kingpins who induced various persons and facilitated procurement of fake arms licences from outside the State of Uttarakhand.

Learned counsel further submitted that charge-sheet has already been filed against similarly situated co-accused persons and that the role attributed to the present applicants is identical to that of the said co-accused persons against whom offences under Sections 318(4) and 61(2) of the B.N.S., 2023 and Section 25(1B)(a) of the Arms Act, 1959 have been invoked. It is contended that, after investigation, the role of the present applicants is confined only to procuring arms licences through Saurabh Agarwal and Satanand Sharma. While investigation against other similarly situated co-accused persons has been concluded, the investigation against the present applicants is still continuing.

4. Learned State Counsel sought four weeks' time to file a counter affidavit.

5. The aforesaid aspects require consideration.

6. Accordingly, as an interim measure, without expressing any opinion on the merits of the case, it is directed that in the event of arrest of the applicants during the course of investigation, they shall be released on interim anticipatory bail on furnishing a_ personal bond along with two reliable sureties each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the Arresting Officer, subject to the following conditions:

(i) The applicants shall cooperate with the investigation and shall appear before the Investigating Officer as and when required;
(ii) The applicants shall not, directly or indirectly, make any inducement, threat or promise to any person acquainted with the facts of the case so as to dissuade such person from disclosing such facts to the Court or to any police officer.

7. List these cases on 03.09.2026.
